How To Use 3DGeoGon

                
            

            

                
Click Add to place a 3-D object, then adjust its dimensions in 3-D Objects.

                
Select labelled vertices and other points in order from Vertices & Points; available actions appear only for valid selections.

                
Shape corners are vertices. Centres, midpoints, intersections, and other constructed locations are points.

                
Use the lists to hide, show, or delete created line segments, triangles, quadrilaterals, angles, and labels.

                
Use INSPECT on triangles, quadrilaterals, and base or section highlights to open an extracted 2-D view for closer analysis.

                
Use 2-D VIEWS to open Front, Top, and Right Side views or an unfolded net of the current solid.

                
If the view gets awkward, use Reset View.

                
Desktop controls: left click + drag rotates, right click + drag pans, mouse wheel zooms, arrow keys rotate, and +/- zoom in/out.

                
On touch devices: drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, and use two-finger drag to pan.

                
For angle notation, ABC means the angle at B.
